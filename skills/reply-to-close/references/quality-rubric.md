# Reply to Close quality rubric

Use this after the gateway has returned a verified artifact during the free public test.

| Required slot | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| `## COPY-PASTE REPLY` | Exactly one reply under 90 words or locale-equivalent; it acknowledges the supplied objection and adds no commercial or factual claim. |
| `## SHORT REPLY` | Exactly one alternative under 40 words or locale-equivalent; it preserves the same supported position and exact one next step. |
| `## OBJECTION CLASSIFICATION` | One concise classification grounded in the inbound objection. |
| `## LOW-FRICTION NEXT STEP` | Exactly one feasible action, identical in both replies, without false urgency. |
| `## ASSUMPTIONS AND TRACEABILITY` | State `None` when there are no gaps, otherwise list the affected phrase and use exactly `Reply claim`, `Input support`, and `Status` columns. Each status is only `Supported`, `Assumption`, or `Placeholder`. |
| `## QUALITY CHECK` | Confirm both length ceilings, the shared single next step, source and quotation fidelity, labeled gaps, no invented commercial/proof claim, and artifact-only guarantee. |

Preserve buyer-supplied names, figures, units and currencies, dates/timeframes, and quotations verbatim. Do not invent a trial, discount, refund, cancellation or no-contract term, feature, proof, ROI, savings, urgency, scarcity, deadline, availability, result, or authority. Do not promise a reply, meeting, booking, purchase, or close.

A public-test evaluation must state that access and gateway response are controlled fixtures and that no real money moved.
